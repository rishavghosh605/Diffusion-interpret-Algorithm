Undoing the noise, fifty times over

What this means for your project

Only two tensors survive from one iteration to the next: t and latents. prompt_embeds, built once in Stage A, is read fifty times and never written. That is the whole reason swapping the conditioning is a single-point intervention: nothing about this loop's control flow has to change, only what line 1045 hands the UNet.

The guidance switch above is the same shape of intervention, one level cruder: it does not touch what the UNet is asked, only how its two answers (with and without the prompt) are combined afterwards, at line 1055.

Diffusion terms

The ideas the math is built from.

Forward process
Adds Gaussian noise to clean data, a little at a time, over \(T=1000\) steps. Survey Eq. 1 to 3. Used to train the UNet, and to derive the reverse step (derivation 3); __call__ never runs it directly.
Reverse process
The learned way back, from noise to data: \(p_\theta(x_{t-1}\mid x_t)\), survey p. 2 and Eq. 6. The denoising loop runs it, fifty times.
Prior
\(\pi(x_T)\), the distribution the reverse process starts from: \(\mathcal N(0,I)\) in the survey. prepare_latents draws one sample of it, before this loop begins.
Noise prediction
\(\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t)\): the UNet's guess of the noise inside \(x_t\). SD 1.5 is trained to predict it, survey Eq. 7. Line 1042 computes it.
Classifier-free guidance (CFG), survey Eq. 13
A way of doing guidance: run the UNet with and without the prompt and push the result away from the unconditional one (Ho and Salimans 2021). Lines 1053 to 1055, and the point of the guidance switch above. Named by contrast with the older classifier guidance (Dhariwal and Nichol 2021), which steers generation using the gradient of a separate classifier trained on noisy images. CFG needs no extra model: the same UNet, called twice, does both halves. The survey's own equation, quoted here so there's nothing to look up: \(\tilde\epsilon_\theta(x,c)=(1+w)\,\epsilon_\theta(x,c)-w\,\epsilon_\theta(x,\phi)\), Eq. 13, survey §2.3 Diffusion Model Guidance. diffusers writes the same thing as \(\epsilon_u+s(\epsilon_c-\epsilon_u)\) with \(s=1+w\); see the notation table.
guidance_rescale
An optional fix for washed-out images at high guidance (Lin et al. 2023), in rescale_noise_cfg, called at lines 1057 to 1059. 0.0 by default, so this branch does not run unless you ask for it.
SNR and terminal SNR
Signal-to-noise ratio, \(\bar\alpha_t/(1-\bar\alpha_t)\): how much image is left at timestep \(t\). Relevant to why guidance_rescale exists (Lin et al. 2023).
\(\eta\) (eta)
A DDIM-only knob (Song, Meng and Ermon 2021), 0 by default in __call__. \(\eta=0\) makes the reverse step deterministic; \(\eta=1\) recovers DDPM's fresh noise at every step. Packed into extra_step_kwargs at step 6, read inside self.scheduler.step.
VP and VE
Two equivalent ways of writing the noising process (Song et al. 2021; survey Eq. 9). Variance preserving (VP) shrinks the signal a little at each step while adding noise, \(x_t=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\epsilon\), so \(\operatorname{Var}(x_t)\) stays near 1 throughout: PNDM, DDIM and DDPM work this way. Variance exploding (VE) never shrinks the signal, only adds noise, \(x+\sigma_t\epsilon\), so the variance grows without bound as \(\sigma_t\) increases: Euler and LMS work this way. This is why scale_model_input is the identity for VP schedulers but has to convert units for VE ones.

Schedulers

Classes in diffusers/schedulers. Each one turns \(\epsilon_\theta\) into the next \(x_{t-1}\) in its own way, inside line 1062; the trained UNet is the same for all of them.

DDPM
Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (Ho et al. 2020). The original model and sampler, Algorithm 2 on this page. Adds fresh noise at every step and is meant for all 1000 steps. Class: DDPMScheduler.
DDIM
Denoising Diffusion Implicit Models (Song, Meng and Ermon 2021). Same trained UNet, but a step that can be deterministic (\(\eta=0\)) and works with about 50 steps. Class: DDIMScheduler.
PNDM
Pseudo Numerical methods for Diffusion Models (Liu et al. 2022). SD 1.5's default. Treats sampling as solving a differential equation and reuses earlier \(\epsilon_\theta\) to take better steps. Accepts neither eta nor generator, so extra_step_kwargs is empty for it. Class: PNDMScheduler.

Models and tools

The other parts of Stable Diffusion that this page mentions.

VAE
Variational autoencoder. An encoder \(\mathcal E\) and decoder \(\mathcal D\). SD 1.5's latents are scaled by 0.18215; vae.decode undoes it after this loop ends, at line 1085.
UNet
The network that predicts \(\epsilon_\theta\): UNet2DConditionModel, called once per iteration at line 1042.
CLIP
The text encoder (Radford et al. 2021), ViT-L/14 for SD 1.5. Turns the prompt into prompt_embeds, shape 77 × 768, once, in Stage A, before this loop starts: 77 is tokenizer.model_max_length, the fixed token count every prompt is padded or truncated to; 768 is CLIP's hidden size, one vector per token position (the encoder's last hidden state, not its pooled output).
fp16
16-bit floating point, torch.float16. The usual precision for running SD 1.5's UNet on a GPU.
