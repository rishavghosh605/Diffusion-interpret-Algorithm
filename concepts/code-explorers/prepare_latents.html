Where it sits in the sampling algorithm

Ho et al. (2020), Algorithm 2, written in the survey's notation. The denoising loop is this algorithm in code.

Lines 4 and 5 are exactly what DDPMScheduler does. PNDM (the default), DDIM and Euler keep lines 1 to 3 and replace lines 4 and 5 with their own update, computed from the same \(\epsilon_\theta\).

What this means for your project

Nothing in these twenty lines reads the prompt. \(x_T\) is fixed by the seed, the image size and the scheduler, and nothing else.

So with a fixed generator, two runs that differ only in prompt_embeds start from the identical \(x_T\). Any difference between the images comes from the conditioning alone, which is what makes swapping the conditioning a clean experiment. Classifier-free guidance doubles the batch later, at line 1037 inside the denoising loop, by copying this same tensor.

Diffusion terms

The ideas the math is built from.

Forward process
Adds Gaussian noise to clean data, a little at a time, over \(T=1000\) steps. Survey Eq. 1 to 3. Used to train the UNet; __call__ never runs it.
Reverse process
The learned way back, from noise to data: \(p_\theta(x_{t-1}\mid x_t)\), survey p. 2 and Eq. 6. The denoising loop runs it.
Prior
\(\pi(x_T)\), the distribution the reverse process starts from: \(\mathcal N(0,I)\) in the survey. prepare_latents draws one sample of it.
Noise schedule
The \(\beta_t\): how much noise each forward step adds. SD 1.5 uses scaled_linear: \(\sqrt{\beta_t}\) runs evenly from \(\sqrt{0.00085}\) to \(\sqrt{0.012}\) over 1000 steps.
Noise prediction
\(\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t)\): the UNet's guess of the noise inside \(x_t\). SD 1.5 is trained to predict it, survey Eq. 7.
SNR and terminal SNR
Signal-to-noise ratio, \(\bar\alpha_t/(1-\bar\alpha_t)\): how much image is left at timestep \(t\). Terminal means at \(t=T\). For SD 1.5 it is about 0.0047, not 0 (Lin et al. 2023).
VP and VE
Two ways to write the same noising (Song et al. 2021; survey Eq. 9). Variance preserving shrinks the image while adding noise, so the total stays near 1: DDPM, DDIM and PNDM work this way. Variance exploding keeps the image and lets the noise level \(\sigma\) grow: Euler and LMS work this way.
σ table
The list of noise levels \(\sigma_t\) for the timesteps of this run. Euler and LMS build it in set_timesteps (step 4) and read init_noise_sigma from it.
Timestep spacing
How the 50 inference timesteps are picked from the 1000 training ones: linspace (evenly, starting at 999), leading or trailing (Lin et al. 2023, Table 2).

Schedulers

Classes in diffusers/schedulers. Each one turns \(\epsilon_\theta\) into the next \(x_{t-1}\) in its own way; the trained UNet is the same for all of them.

DDPM
Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (Ho et al. 2020). The original model and sampler, Algorithm 2 on this page. Adds fresh noise at every step and is meant for all 1000 steps. Class: DDPMScheduler.
DDIM
Denoising Diffusion Implicit Models (Song, Meng and Ermon 2021). Same trained UNet, but a step that can be deterministic (\(\eta=0\)) and works with about 50 steps. Class: DDIMScheduler. The X-Ray reads its reverse step.
PNDM
Pseudo Numerical methods for Diffusion Models (Liu et al. 2022). SD 1.5's default. Treats sampling as solving a differential equation and reuses earlier \(\epsilon_\theta\) to take better steps. SD 1.5's config sets skip_prk_steps, so it runs the PLMS (pseudo linear multistep) variant. VP units, so init_noise_sigma is 1. Class: PNDMScheduler.
Euler
One step of Euler's method per timestep, on the differential equation written in VE units (Karras et al. 2022). init_noise_sigma is 14.61 on SD 1.5. Class: EulerDiscreteScheduler.
LMS
Linear multistep: like Euler, in VE units, but combines several earlier \(\epsilon_\theta\) per step. Class: LMSDiscreteScheduler.

Models and tools

The other parts of Stable Diffusion that this page mentions.

Latent diffusion
Running diffusion on a VAE's compressed latents instead of on pixels (Rombach et al. 2022). Stable Diffusion (SD) is a latent diffusion model; SD 1.5 is the version this page reads.
VAE
Variational autoencoder. An encoder \(\mathcal E\) that compresses an image and a decoder \(\mathcal D\) that restores it. SD 1.5's maps 3 × 512 × 512 to 4 × 64 × 64; latents are scaled by 0.18215.
UNet
The network that predicts \(\epsilon_\theta\): UNet2DConditionModel, convolutions with attention layers, shaped like a U (Ronneberger et al. 2015).
CLIP
The text encoder (Radford et al. 2021; SD 1.5 uses ViT-L/14's). Turns the prompt into prompt_embeds, 77 × 768, in step 3.
Classifier-free guidance (CFG)
Run the UNet with and without the prompt and push the result away from the unconditional one (Ho and Salimans 2021; survey Eq. 13). It is why the batch doubles at line 1037.
guidance_rescale
An optional fix for washed-out images at high guidance (Lin et al. 2023), in rescale_noise_cfg near the top of the pipeline file.
fp16
16-bit floating point, torch.float16. Half the memory of 32-bit; the usual way to run SD 1.5 on a GPU.
NCHW
The order of a PyTorch image tensor's axes: batch, channels, height, width.
